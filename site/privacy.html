Privacy

Stephen has no server. Nothing is sent to us.

Stephen keeps its data in ~/.stephen, in folders and files only you can read, unless your config says otherwise.

What leaves your Mac

  • The text of your command, and what Stephen reads to answer it (a page, an email), go to Claude through your own Claude account.
  • Calls to accounts you connect, such as Gmail or Canvas, are made from your Mac.
  • Your audio is not uploaded. The wake word and transcription run on your Mac.

Crash reports

A crash record holds times, versions and code words only. It holds no words you said, web addresses, page text, names or audio. It is sent only when you run stephen report and type y after reading it.

This website
