Privacy

Stephen has no server. Nothing is sent to us.

Your voice

Stays on your Mac. Speech to text runs there.

Sent to Claude

Your request as text, and what Stephen reads to answer it, through your own account.

Your passwords

Stay in the macOS Keychain. Never shown to the model.

What stays on your Mac

Most of it lives in ~/.stephen, readable only by you unless your config says otherwise. Job output is in ~/Stephen Jobs. Claude Code keeps transcripts in its own folder.

Spoken replies

Stephen speaks with a voice on your Mac. If you add an ElevenLabs key, it uses ElevenLabs first, so the reply text goes to ElevenLabs. Remove the key with stephen voice-key delete.

Crash reports

A crash record holds times, versions and code words only. It holds none of your words, web addresses, page text, names or audio. It is sent only when you run stephen report and type y after reading it.

This website

This site is plain pages on GitHub Pages, with no tracking, no cookies and nothing loaded from other sites. GitHub may keep its own server logs.
