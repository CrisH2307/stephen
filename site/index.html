Stephen is a voice assistant for your Mac and browser. It asks before anything risky.

Say "Hey Stephen, open YouTube." Or hold F5 and talk.

Stephen is in a friends beta. It is early, and some things are slow. How to get the beta.

Voice control of your Mac and browser

  • Say "Hey Stephen", or hold F5, then speak.
  • Stephen works in your own Zen or Chrome, signed in as you.
  • It can do several steps from one sentence, like "what's due on Canvas this week, and put on some lofi".
  • It answers in one or two spoken sentences.
  • It can read Canvas, your calendar, Notion and Gmail. These reads are read-only.
  • Honest limit: "Hey Stephen" is slow today (about 9.6 seconds, median). Holding F5 is faster (about 2 seconds, median).

It asks first, and page text is never an order

  • Risky actions stop at a Mac dialog. A spoken "yes" never counts. Allow and Deny are clicks only.
  • Web pages, emails and Canvas posts are treated as data. The model cannot write memory or timers. It can start a job only from the phone link, after you approve the exact task on your phone.
  • After Stephen reads something private, it asks before it sends anything out.
  • The wake word gets less trust than F5, because a video could say "Hey Stephen".

Stephen is designed and tested to resist prompt injection. It has thousands of automated tests, an attack test with a real model, and independent reviews. We do not claim it cannot be tricked. The open safety items are listed in the repo.

Your speech stays on your Mac

  • The wake word, end-of-sentence detection and transcription run on your Mac. Your audio does not leave it.
  • Claude gets the text of your command, plus what Stephen reads to answer it (a page, an email).
  • Nothing is sent to us. Stephen has no server.
  • Passwords and tokens stay in the macOS Keychain and are never shown to the model.

The full list of what is stored, for how long, and how to delete it is on the privacy page.

What Stephen is not

  • Not a chatbot. One spoken request, one short spoken answer.
  • Not for Windows or Linux. It needs macOS 14 or newer.
  • Not one-click yet. Setup is a few Terminal commands and three Mac permission screens.
  • Not free of cost. It runs on your own Claude plan.
  • Not signed or notarized yet. After an update, macOS may ask for permissions again.
  • Not an official Anthropic product. The AI behind Stephen is Claude.
