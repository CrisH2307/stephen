# Market scan: Mac voice and computer-use assistants (Nov 2026)

Updates the table in `docs/research/PRODUCT-PLAN-2026-11.md` section 6. Today is 2026-11-25.

**Method.** Standard web search, 8 queries on 2026-11-25. Sources are news sites, vendor pages and blogs, not
audits. I did not open every vendor page. Marks: **[ok]** two or more sources agree, **[one]** one source,
**[?]** uncertain or not found. Facts about Stephen come from `docs/NFR-STEPHEN.md` and the README only.
Check prices on the vendor page before you quote them.

## 1. Short answer

- All big players now have voice plus "do things on your Mac". Voice alone is no longer a reason to pick Stephen.
- Their safety is mostly "ask before each app" plus model training. None of the sources I read publishes a
  prompt-injection pass rate. Stephen has a code gate and an eval (`stephen eval`).
- Most of them cost money or need a paid plan. Stephen is free and uses the friend's own Claude account.
- Their audio and screen context go to the cloud. Stephen keeps speech to text on the Mac.

## 2. Competitors

### Siri AI and Apple Intelligence (macOS 27 "Golden Gate")

- **What it does.** New Siri with personal context, on-screen awareness and app actions through App Intents; a
  standalone Siri app. Apple says it uses Google Gemini models for its foundation models. A MacStories review
  disputes the "powered by Gemini" framing **[?]**.
  Sources: [MacRumors](https://www.macrumors.com/2026/06/08/apple-unveils-siri-app/),
  [TNW](https://thenextweb.com/news/apple-wwdc-2026-siri-ai-gemini-ios-27),
  [MacStories](https://www.macstories.net/stories/macos-27-the-macstories-review/6/).
- **Status.** One Macworld piece says macOS 27 arrived on 14 Sep **[one]**
  ([Macworld](https://www.macworld.com/article/3139330/macos-27-golden-gate-has-arrived-heres-what-s-new-on-your-mac.html)).
  Siri AI is still called a beta. English first, more languages from October **[one]**
  ([Digital Trends](https://www.digitaltrends.com/?p=6007136)). Needs Apple silicon.
- **Safety and privacy.** On-device plus Private Cloud Compute; Apple says data is not visible to Apple or third
  parties. Critics doubt it, and one review says the privacy limits hold back cross-app power
  ([Gadget Review](https://www.gadgetreview.com/?p=385569)). A review says there is no "agent mode" yet
  ([Macworld](https://www.macworld.com/article/3280358/agentic-siri-is-coming-and-thats-just-the-beginning.html)).
- **Price.** The macOS upgrade is free ([MacPaw](https://macpaw.com/reviews/new-macos-golden-gate)). Usage limits: the
  Nov plan mentions daily limits in the beta; I could not confirm it **[?]**.
- **Stephen is different.** Stephen runs on macOS 14 and up, not only 27. It reaches Canvas, Notion, Gmail and Zen
  with taint tracking and Allow/Deny clicks. Siri is the better tool for timers, weather and simple app actions;
  Stephen should not fight there.

### ChatGPT desktop (Voice, Computer Use, Agent)

- **What it does.** On 23 Jul 2026 OpenAI added Voice (GPT-Live) to the Mac and Windows app. It can steer work
  in Chat, Work and Codex and use Computer Use, local files and plugins. On Mac, "Appshots" lets it see the
  focused window. Sources: [9to5Mac](https://9to5mac.com/2026/07/23/openai-updating-chatgpt-desktop-app-with-gpt-voice-for-talking-through-work/),
  [Android Authority](https://androidauthority.com/openai-chatgpt-voice-desktop-rollout-3691031).
- **Safety and privacy.** Voice follows the same permissions as the task it directs. I found no clear voice-audio
  retention statement **[?]**. A separate opt-in "Computer History" feature is off by default for Pro, Business
  and Enterprise and has drawn privacy criticism ([Inc.](https://www.inc.com/moses-jeanfrancois/openai-wants-chatgpt-to-learn-how-you-work-raises-privacy-questions/91391800)).
- **Price.** The Mac app is free. Voice is for Plus, Pro, Business, Edu and Enterprise, not Free, with a rolling
  5-hour allowance ([Android Authority](https://androidauthority.com/openai-chatgpt-voice-desktop-rollout-3691031)).
  Current monthly prices **[?]**, not confirmed.
- **Stephen is different.** No wake word found in the coverage **[?]**. Cloud audio. Safety is permissions plus
  the model. Stephen has local speech and a code gate that the model cannot change.

### Claude desktop: computer use, Dispatch

- **What it does.** Since 23 Mar 2026 Claude can use the Mac (click, type, see the screen) in Cowork and Claude
  Code. Dispatch sends tasks from a phone to the Mac. Windows followed in April **[one]**
  ([botmonster](https://botmonster.com/ai/claude-computer-use-hands-on-ai-desktop-control-cowork/)).
  Anthropic pages: [blog](https://claude.com/blog/dispatch-and-computer-use),
  [support](https://support.claude.com/en/articles/14128542-let-claude-use-your-computer-in-cowork).
- **Safety and privacy.** Asks before each app. Some app types (trading, crypto) are blocked by default. Needs
  Accessibility and Screen Recording. Anthropic says it trains Claude to spot injection and adds outside
  safeguards. It advises against use on apps with health or finance data. A PCWorld reviewer called a
  30-minute run "worrisome" ([PCWorld](https://www.pcworld.com/article/3097542/claude-controlled-my-mac-for-half-an-hour-it-was-a-wild-worrisome-ride.html)).
- **Price.** Paid plans only: Pro and Max, not Team or Enterprise at launch. Pro is $20 a month. Max is reported
  as $100 and $200 tiers; the pricing page text was garbled, so **[?]**
  ([Anthropic pricing](https://claude.com/pricing)). Support pages say Cowork is now just "Claude", so details may
  have moved **[?]**.
- **Stephen is different.** Stephen is built on Claude Code, so it needs the friend's Claude sign-in anyway. It
  adds an always-on wake word, spoken replies, and a deterministic gate. It does not take over the whole screen;
  it uses browser and app tools. This is the closest product, so keep the story clear: Claude desktop is a
  general computer operator, Stephen is a voice front door with a safety gate.

## 3. Where Stephen is different

| Area | Others | Stephen |
|---|---|---|
| Wake word | Siri yes. ChatGPT and Claude desktop: not seen. | Yes ("Hey Stephen" and F5). |
| Safety | Ask-per-app and model training. | Code gate: policy, taint, egress, rate limit, native Allow/Deny. Model cannot write memory, timers or jobs. |
| Proof | No published injection pass rate found. | `stephen eval --category inject`, 100% target per release. |
| Privacy | Cloud audio or screen context in most. | Speech to text on the Mac. Nothing sent to us. |
| Price | Paid plans or beta. | Free; uses the friend's own Claude account and a daily cost limit. |
| Accounts | Vendor accounts first. | Canvas, Notion, multi-account Gmail, Zen. |
| Weak spots | n/a | Needs Claude Code installed, setup is not yet 15 minutes, English only, Mac only, wake word replies are slow (NFR section 3). |

## 4. Top findings

1. **Voice plus computer use is now normal.** Apple, OpenAI and Anthropic all ship it. Stephen must sell safety,
   privacy and cost, not "you can talk to your Mac".
2. **Nobody publishes injection results.** A short public eval page is the cheapest way to stand out. It also
   matches the earlier hiring research in `docs/RESEARCH-MARKET.md`.
3. **Claude desktop is the closest rival and shares the engine.** Make the difference plain on the site: always
   listening, spoken, gated by code, free on your own account.
