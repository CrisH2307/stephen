How it works

Four steps from your voice to a finished task.

  1. You speak

    "Hey Stephen", or hold F5. Your speech becomes text on your Mac.

  2. Claude plans

    The model can only use Stephen's own tools. It has no other way to touch your Mac.

  3. The gate checks

    Fixed rules look at each action: allowed, ask you first, or never.

  4. Stephen answers

    It tells you what it did, out loud and in writing, with every step listed.

The rules

The model can ask. Only the gate can say yes.

An AI model can be tricked by a web page or an email. So the model is not the one that decides. A small piece of ordinary code is, and it gives the same answer every time.

  • A spoken "yes" never counts

    Sending, submitting, paying and deleting need a click on Allow, next to the exact question. Your voice cannot approve them, and neither can a recording of it.

  • Private data is tracked

    When Stephen reads something private, like your mail, the gate remembers. After that it blocks actions that could carry the data somewhere else.

  • Web pages and emails are data

    Text that Stephen reads is never treated as an order. It is shown to you as plain text, exactly as written.

  • Everything is on the record

    Each step and each refusal is listed under the reply, with where the command came from: typed, F5, "Hey Stephen" or your phone.

Questions

Short answers.

Does my voice leave my Mac?

No. Your speech is turned into text on your Mac. The text of your request then goes to Claude so it can plan the work. Spoken replies can use an online voice or a voice on your Mac; you choose in Settings.

What if a web page tells Stephen to do something?

Stephen treats page and email text as information, not as orders. And even if the model were fooled, the gate still checks every action against the same fixed rules.

Can I say "yes" to approve?

No. Approving is a click on Allow, shown next to the exact question. The buttons wait a moment before they work, so a stray click does not approve by accident.

Do I need another subscription?

Stephen uses the Claude plan you already have. There is no extra Stephen subscription today.

When can I try it?

Not yet. Stephen is finishing its core functions first. Sharing it with other people comes after that.
