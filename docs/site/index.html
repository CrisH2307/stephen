A voice assistant for your Mac that cannot go past your rules.

Say "Hey Stephen" or hold F5. It works in your own browser and school accounts. Anything that submits or sends waits for your click.

Not available to download yet.

  1. 1

    Hold F5 and speak.

    Or say "Hey Stephen". Every message shows where it came from.

  2. 2

    Your words become text on your Mac.

    Speech to text runs on the Mac. The text of your request goes to Claude to plan the work.

  3. 3

    Claude plans with Stephen's own tools.

    The model can only use the tools Stephen gives it. It has no other way to touch your Mac.

  4. 4

    The gate checks every step.

    Fixed rules decide, not the model: allowed, asks you, or never.

  5. 5

    Submitting waits for your click.

    Allow and Deny wait a moment, so a stray click cannot approve. A spoken yes never counts.
